Madhukeshwara: Deity & Significance

The Madhukeshwara shrine and its Shaiva identity in Banavasi.

The temple is dedicated to Madhukeshwara, a form of Shiva worshipped in the principal shrine. Karnataka Tourism describes the linga as honey-coloured. The temple’s name, devotional practice and sculptural setting together express a living Shaiva tradition in a historic sacred landscape.

Visitors are asked to follow the temple’s customary rules around worship and photography. Details about darshan sequence, sanctum access, offerings and ritual timings must be confirmed locally; this site does not prescribe them.

Nandi

A carved Nandi is among the notable features documented in photographs of the temple. Nandi is traditionally associated with Shiva and faces the principal shrine. The image shown here is a view of the temple’s Nandi, not a substitute for darshan.
