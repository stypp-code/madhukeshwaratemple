Temple Architecture

Explore the layered architecture, carved stonework and notable features of Madhukeshwara Temple.

The architecture at Banavasi is layered rather than the product of a single building campaign. ASI publications describe an older sanctum with Kadamba features and later additions and renovations; the complex also contains work associated with subsequent periods. The exact dating and terminology of individual components should be presented with reference to specialist documentation.

Stonework and the mantapa

The temple precinct is known for its carved pillars, sculptural details and the Triloka Mantapa. Photographs in this site’s gallery show the entrance, gopura, mantapa and Nandi. A stone cot preserved in the precinct is discussed in the ASI historical account, which transcribes an inscription and gives a probable date of 1628.

Conservation

The temple is a protected heritage monument. Visitors should avoid touching carvings, climbing on structures or leaving offerings outside designated areas. Current conservation work and access restrictions should be confirmed with the relevant authorities.
