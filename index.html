Welcome to Banavasi

Shri Madhukeshwara Temple

Set in Banavasi, a historic centre associated with the Kadamba dynasty, Shri Madhukeshwara Temple is a living Shaiva shrine surrounded by a layered architectural past. The temple precinct brings together carved stonework, sacred spaces and features shaped across successive periods.

This site gathers historical context, visitor guidance and photographs with their source credits. Operational details are included only when verified.
