A welcome to Banavasi

An enduring place of worship and heritage

Set in Banavasi, an important historic centre of the Kadambas, Shri Madhukeshwara Temple brings together a living Shaiva tradition and a layered architectural past. Its sanctum, carved stonework and surrounding precinct tell a story shaped across centuries.

This portal brings together careful historical context, visitor guidance and credited photographs. Service details are shown only when verified.
