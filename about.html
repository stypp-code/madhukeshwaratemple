About the Temple

An introduction to Shri Madhukeshwara Temple and historic Banavasi.

Shri Madhukeshwara Temple is a historic Shaiva shrine in Banavasi, Uttara Kannada district, Karnataka. It stands within a town whose recorded history reaches across many centuries and whose heritage includes layers of Hindu and Buddhist material culture.

The temple is a place of worship and a significant architectural landmark. Its visible fabric reflects successive periods of construction, patronage and renewal. This website presents the history with care and keeps practical details unpublished until they are confirmed by the temple administration.
