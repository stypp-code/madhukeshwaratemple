# Madhukeshwara Temple image inventory

**Research date:** 7 November 2026  
**Collection status:** Partial — 22 of 35 eligible Commons photographs have been downloaded and converted to WebP. The remaining 13 are documented below as pending and are not yet available to the site.

The verified source set was identified in the [Wikimedia Commons temple category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Madhukesvara_Temple,_Banavasi) and its [Nandi subcategory](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Nandi_at_the_Madhukesvara_Temple,_Banavasi). The separate `Ardha Ganapati Temple, Banavasi.jpg` photograph was excluded because it depicts another shrine. Each Commons file page is linked as the source record and provides the original file URL, creator, and license. The nine local originals are archived in `assets/temple-originals/`; optimized WebP derivatives are in `public/images/temple/gallery/` (EXIF orientation applied, maximum dimension 2400 px, quality 88).

For each downloaded derivative, credit the named photographer, link the Commons file page and the stated license, and indicate that the image was resized/recompressed. For CC BY-SA images, share adaptations under the same or a compatible license. The pending rows are rights-cleared candidates, not collected assets; check their file page again before downloading or publication.

## Image gaps and discovery sources

- Rows 1–22 are local WebP assets. Rows 23–35 are not downloaded and must not be referenced as local files yet.
- No verified festival or religious ceremony photograph was found in the inspected Commons categories. No separately verified close view of the main linga was identified. These remain gaps; no unrelated or AI-generated image is substituted.
- The [HRCE Karnataka virtual tour](https://itms.kar.nic.in/hrcehome/hrce_virtualtour.php?tid=346) was reviewed as a discovery source, but no reuse license was identified for its photos, so they were not copied.
- The [NativePlanet gallery](https://www.nativeplanet.com/banavasi/photos/) was reviewed. Its Commons-attributed photos are sourced through the Commons entries above; other hosted photos had no reuse grant visible on the gallery page and were not copied.
- The [Flickr album](https://www.flickr.com/photos/srinathholla/albums/72157623117814241/) was reviewed, but the individual photo rights could not be verified, so no Flickr-hosted images were downloaded.
- The remaining 13 originals could not be fetched in this session: Wikimedia download requests timed out, and the project shell later lost DNS access to Wikimedia. This inventory is therefore a documented partial collection, not completion of the requested 35–50 local images.
