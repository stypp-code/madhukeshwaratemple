Donations

Donation information for Shri Madhukeshwara Temple; authorised payment channels are pending verification.

Support for the temple should be offered only through channels confirmed by the temple administration. No bank account, UPI ID, payment gateway or online donation facility has been verified for this site.

Protect your contribution: Do not send money using details copied from an unofficial page. Ask the temple office to confirm the authorised process and obtain a receipt.

Online donations

Payment collection is disabled. An approved payment provider, official beneficiary details, receipt workflow, privacy terms and refund policy are required before donations can be accepted online.
